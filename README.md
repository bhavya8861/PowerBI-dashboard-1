# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales and profit performance for a retail superstore, built to help stakeholders track KPIs, identify trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

![Dashboard Preview](superstore_screenshot.png)

## Overview

This dashboard analyzes **$12.6M in total sales** and **$1.47M in total profit** across a 4-year period, breaking down performance by category, region, segment, and sub-category to surface actionable business insights.

## Key Features

- **KPI Cards** — Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity, and Profit Margin at a glance
- **Sales Trend Analysis** — Monthly trend line showing seasonal patterns and growth over time
- **Category & Sub-Category Breakdown** — Sales and profit performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies
- **Regional Analysis** — Sorted bar chart showing sales performance across all regions
- **Segment Analysis** — Performance comparison across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments
- **Interactive Filters** — Dynamic slicers for Year and Region to enable self-service data exploration

## Key Insights

- **Technology** is the highest-performing category by both sales ($4.7M) and profit ($0.66M)
- **Central region** drives the highest regional sales at $2.8M
- **Consumer segment** generates the most revenue at $6.5M, nearly double the Corporate segment
- Overall **profit margin** stands at **11.62%**

## Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard design and data modeling
- **DAX** — custom measures for profit margin and aggregations
- **Data Cleaning & Formatting** — continuous date axis, sorted categorical charts, label formatting for readability

## Dataset

Sample Superstore dataset (Sales, Profit, Category, Region, Segment, Order Date, and related fields).
